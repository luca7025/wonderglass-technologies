# WOODLAND • Architecture Firm Website

A clean, modern, and responsive architecture firm website built with **React 19**, **TypeScript**, and **Vite**, replicating the Woodland Architecture Firm Webflow design template across all 10 screenshots.

---

## 🌟 Sections Implemented

1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - `WOODLAND` brand mark + `Spaces like home since 2010` tagline.
   - Smooth navigation menu: `About`, `Services`, `Projects`, `Gallery`, `Reviews`, `Contact`.
   - Mobile hamburger drawer navigation.

2. **Hero Section**:
   - Giant bold backdrop text: `WOODLAND`.
   - Architectural photography of a modern luxury white villa with infinity pool and glass balconies.

3. **About Section**:
   - Dual-layout grid with bold `ABOUT` heading and 2-column editorial copy.

4. **Services Section (Accordion)**:
   - Interactive expand / collapse services with `+` and `−` toggles:
     - **01 Architecture**: Concept Design, Architectural Planning, 3D Modeling, Construction Documentation + paired architecture pavilion photography.
     - **02 Interior Design**: Spatial Identity, Material & Finish Selection, Furniture & Lighting Design, Detail Development + paired interior living space photography.
     - **03 Layout Planning**: Functional Zoning, Human-Centered Design, Circulation Strategy, Flexibility & Future Use + paired double-height room photography.
     - **04 Project Management**: Stakeholder Coordination, Quality Control, Timeline & Budget Tracking + paired on-site architects photography.

5. **Projects Section**:
   - Stacked architectural project showcases:
     - **Coastal Villa** (*Aspen • Residential • 2024*) with bottom-right floating frosted glass info card.
     - **The Willow Loft** (*Aspen • Residential • 2024*) with bottom-left floating frosted glass info card.
     - **Sunshine Retreat** (*Aspen • Residential • 2024*) with bottom-right floating frosted glass info card.

6. **By The Numbers & As Featured In**:
   - Statistics: `316+` projects completed, `740+` satisfied customers, `215+` YTD transactions.
   - Brand features: **HGTV**, **eXp REALTY**, **FORTUNE MAGAZINE**.

7. **Gallery Section**:
   - Giant backdrop title: `GALLERY`.
   - 5-piece asymmetric architectural & interior design collage.

8. **Reviews Section (Dark Mode)**:
   - Dark `#141416` contrast section.
   - `HEAR OUR CLIENT` kicker + `REVIEWS` title.
   - Interactive testimonial quote carousel with `←` and `→` rounded navigation controls.

9. **Partner With Us (Contact Form)**:
   - Full name, Email address, and Project description inputs with custom submit state.
   - Paired minimalist white curved architectural photography.

10. **Footer**:
    - Quick Links, Resources, Contact (`hi@woodland.com`, Twitter, LinkedIn), Newsletter subscription form.
    - Legal links (`Privacy Policy`, `Cookie Policy`).
